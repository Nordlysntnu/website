# Board

The Board coordinates the team and its technical and non-technical work.

## Board Member

Tell us if you are interested in contributing to the Board. This is a general expression of interest and does not mean that a specific Board position is currently available.

# Business

The Business group works with partners, sponsorships and the team's finances.

## Business Member

Join the Business group and contribute to partner relationships, sponsorships or financial planning.

# Marketing

The Marketing group shares Nordlys' work and builds awareness of the team.

## Marketing Member

Join the Marketing group and contribute to communication, content, events or recruitment activities.

# Logistics

The Logistics group plans the practical work around competitions, transport and team activities.

## Logistics Member

Join the Logistics group and contribute to planning, transport or practical arrangements.

# Aero & Composites

The Aero & Composites group works on the car's aerodynamics, body and composite structures.

## Aero & Composites Member

Join the Aero & Composites group and contribute to design, analysis or practical production work.

# Mechanical

The Mechanical group develops and builds the car's mechanical systems.

## Mechanical Member

Join the Mechanical group and contribute to design, analysis, production or testing of mechanical systems.

# Electrical

The Electrical group develops the car's electrical and electronic systems.

## Electrical Member

Join the Electrical group and contribute to electrical systems, electronics or embedded development.

# Software

The Software group develops software for the team, including strategy tools, telemetry and the website.

## Software Member

Join the Software group and contribute to software development, data analysis or the website.
